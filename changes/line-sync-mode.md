Sync whole lines with a per-song Line timing mode (experimental)

Behind **Preferences › Editor & sync › Line syncing**, off by default. In Line mode, F starts a line, G ends it and starts the next, H ends it, and the TTML exports every line line-synced. Switching back to Word keeps the line times, and word syncing then stays inside them. LRC files open in Line mode while the option is on.
