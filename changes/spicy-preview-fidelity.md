Spicy preview matches Spicy Lyrics more closely

In **Simple Lyrics mode**, words now fill at the same pace and moment as in Spicy Lyrics. Invisible characters such as zero-width spaces no longer show up as empty words or letters, and a font you pick in Appearance now applies to the Spicy preview too; with the default font it keeps Spicy Lyrics' own font.
