Clear a word's timing with a middle-click

In Time mode, middle-click a word or syllable to clear its start and end times. Undo brings them back. Clicks that start, steer or stop scrolling are ignored. Change or remove the shortcut in Preferences → Shortcuts, where it can be a mouse button with modifier keys or a keyboard shortcut that clears the selected words.
