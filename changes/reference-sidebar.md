Reference sidebar with the preview and Genius lyrics in Edit and Time mode

A resizable panel docks on the right with the Spicy, Standard or Toxi preview and the song's Genius lyrics, and remembers its tab and width for each project. It's behind a flag: turn on **Reference sidebar** in Preferences › Editor. It replaces the Preview panel while it's on.
