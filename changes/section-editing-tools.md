Edit sections after import and reuse a chorus's timings

Turn on **Section editing tools** in Preferences › Editor & sync (off by default while it's tested). Click a section header to rename or retype it, use **Start section here** on any line, move where a section starts, and use **Apply timings from...** to copy a synced chorus onto the next one: only matching words get timings, so check them afterwards. Opening an Apple-style TTML also reads its song parts as sections.
