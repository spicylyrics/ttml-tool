The spectrogram can follow the playhead

Turn it on in **Preferences → Spectrogram** (off by default while it's tested). **Page** jumps ahead when the playhead leaves the view, **Continuous** keeps it centered. Scrolling or dragging pauses it until you press play or leave it for a moment. Toggle it from the spectrogram toolbar or with `Shift+P`.
