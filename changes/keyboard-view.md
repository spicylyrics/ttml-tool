Keyboard view and second keys for shortcuts (experimental)

Turn on **Keyboard view and second keys** in Preferences › Shortcuts to see your shortcuts on a keyboard, colored by sync, edit, playback and navigation. Click a key to change it, give an action a second key (say, Z as well as F for Start) or reset it, and search to light up an action's keys. Off by default; with it off, shortcuts work exactly as before.
