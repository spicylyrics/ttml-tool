See and hear word timing boundaries

An optional timing bar fills during each lead or duet word or timed syllable, marks timed syllable boundaries, goes dark in gaps, and counts down to the next word or syllable. Optional clicks can cue whole-word starts and ends or each timed syllable, following the audio at any playback speed with a separate volume control. Music volume and equalizer settings do not affect cue volume. Background vocal cues are quieter and duet cues use a distinct higher tone. Turn on the bar or clicks using the clock button beside playback or in Audio settings.
