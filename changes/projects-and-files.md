To sync song list, start screen and text export in Projects

Turn on **Song list and start screen** in Preferences → Files & storage (off by default). The TTML checklist becomes a **To sync** tab in Projects: add songs by typing them or searching Genius, and **Start project** opens a fresh document with the title and artist filled in, marked done when you export it. The empty editor shows recent projects, the list and ways to start; **Scan folder** offers to link same-name TTML and audio files as projects; dropping a TTML and its audio together opens both; launch asks to continue your last session; and File → Export lyrics gains plain text and Genius text, plus **Copy as Genius text**.
