Edit the whole lyrics as text and keep your timings

**Edit > Edit as text...** opens the lyrics in the import screen's text editor, with sections, background and duet lines, syllable splits and translations. Applying keeps the timing of every word you didn't change, shows what changes first, and undoes in one step. Behind a switch for now: Preferences > Files > **Edit as text**.
