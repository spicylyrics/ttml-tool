Short tips when you do something the slow way

After you sync three repeated lines by hand, a tip points to **Duplicate at playhead**; after leaving Time mode twice just to split a word, a tip shows the Ctrl+double-click split. Each tip shows once, never while you're syncing with playback running, and **Don't show tips** turns them off. It is off for now: turn on **Show tips** in Preferences > Editor.
