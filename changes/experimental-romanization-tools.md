Experimental romanization and splitting tools

Behind **Preferences → Workflow → Experimental romanization tools** (off by default): a rule-based English splitter, a Japanese split size (syllable, mora or word) that keeps small kana, ー and っ with their sound, **Paste romanized...** to fill per-word romanization from a romanized lyric page, and an optional Apple-style head-level translation export.
