Transcribe tab for typing lyrics while the song plays

Turn on **Transcribe tab** in Preferences → Editor & sync (off by default). Type the lyrics as plain text with the audio bar, playback speed and spectrogram below, play, seek and slow down without leaving the text box, export as Genius-formatted or plain text, and send the text to the import screen.
