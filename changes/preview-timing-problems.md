Spicy preview can show words with timing problems

Turn on **Show timing problems** in Preferences → Workflow → Preview (off by default) and the Spicy preview marks words that start before their line, end after it, end before they start, or have no timing, in red with a dashed outline and a tooltip. A count above the preview selects the first one in Time mode.
