Check your lyrics against the Spicy Lyrics review bot before you submit

**Tools → Problems** lists what the review bot would report for your export, grouped into errors, warnings and hints, with a link to the guide for each rule. Select one to jump to its line. Saving or exporting with errors asks first; warnings show one toast that opens the list. The bot's messages stay in English so they match what reviewers see.
